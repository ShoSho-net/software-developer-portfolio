01 — About

From the stage to the screen

I study Theatre Art at the University of Ibadan, where I've learned how to tell a story, hold an audience, and bring an idea to life on stage. Those same instincts — empathy for the audience, attention to detail, and the discipline of rehearsing until it's right — are what drew me to software.

Now I'm channelling that creativity into building things people can actually use. I'm teaching myself web development from the ground up: how the web works, how to write clean code, and how to turn a blank screen into a working product.

I'm just getting started, and I'm documenting the journey here. This site itself is one of my first steps — and it will grow as I do.
